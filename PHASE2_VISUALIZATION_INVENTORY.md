## Data packaging decision

Phase 2 exports compact `housing-eda.json` and `model-diagnostics.json`, plus five-state county geometry and one lazy-loadable PUMA geometry file per state. Existing SHAP payloads remain separate and are fetched only inside the analytical workspace. Figures without saved numerical summaries remain inventoried but are not recreated from pixels or approximated.
